About Thangamagal

A gold-loan service built on trust

Thangamagal Gold Loan is a customer-focused gold-loan finance service located at Chithra Complex, Chatram Bus Stand, Tiruchirappalli. We work to make gold-backed finance simple, transparent, and accessible for the local community.

Every customer who walks in is treated with respect and clarity — from evaluation to disbursement. Our team is on hand to guide you through the process and answer any questions along the way.

  • Transparent, easy-to-understand process
  • Secure handling of pledged gold
  • Professional, courteous assistance
  • Conveniently located near Chatram Bus Stand
